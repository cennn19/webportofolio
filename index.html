01 Point of Sale 2024

WarkopPOS

A small coffee shop in Malang was doing all their orders on paper. End of day, the owner would manually tally receipts — sometimes getting home past midnight just to know if they made a profit.

I built WarkopPOS: a lightweight POS system tailored for Indonesian warung & café scale. Cashier interface on the front, a live dashboard for the owner on the back. Receipt printing, daily summary, stock alerts — the stuff that actually matters for a 3-table coffee shop, not enterprise bloat.

Result: Owner now closes books in under 5 minutes. Revenue transparency led them to drop 2 slow-moving menu items and increase net margin by ~18%.

03 Secondhand Marketplace + Auction 2025

Barkos

Students in Indonesian boarding houses ("kos") accumulate stuff — furniture, appliances, books — and then move out. Most just leave it behind because selling is a hassle. There had to be a better way within the kos community itself.

Barkos is a secondhand goods marketplace built specifically for boarding house communities. Sellers list items, buyers browse by kos location, and high-demand items go to real-time auction. WebSocket-powered bidding, escrow-style payment flow, and an admin panel for kos managers.

Result: Piloting in 4 kos in Malang. 80+ items listed in first month, Rp 3.2jt in transactions facilitated.

04 Landing Page 2024

Panen Digital

An agri-tech startup needed to validate their concept before building the full product. They had a pitch deck and 3 weeks before a demo day with investors.

I built a high-converting landing page with a waitlist form, problem/solution narrative, and animated product preview. Integrated with Mailchimp for the waitlist, Hotjar for heatmaps, and Google Analytics for conversion tracking. Lighthouse score: 97.

Result: 340 waitlist signups in 3 weeks. Investors funded the next round. The page paid for itself before demo day.
